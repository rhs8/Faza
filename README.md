Faza! Remember Everything You Love

Because the music that defined your summer of 2019 deserves to be remembered.

What is Faza?
Faza is a personal interest tracker and social discovery app that lets you catalogue everything you love, movies, music, books, food, fashion, games, and more, so your taste is never lost to time.
As we get older, the things that shaped us quietly fade. That album you had on repeat. The movie you watched three times. The restaurant you swore you'd go back to. Faza keeps all of it, organized and time-stamped, so you can look back at any moment in your life and remember exactly what you were into.
The Problem
Memory is lossy. We consume more than ever, shows, music, books, food, but we retain almost none of it in any organized way. There's no single place to track:

The movie you loved but can't remember the name of
The band you were obsessed with two years ago
The book a friend recommended that you keep forgetting to read
The restaurant you discovered on a trip

Faza solves this.
Features
Your Vault
Create categories around your interests, as broad or niche as you want.
CategoryExamplesFilm & TVMovies, shows, documentariesMusicArtists, albums, playlistsBooksNovels, non-fiction, graphic novelsFoodRestaurants, dishes, cuisinesFashionBrands, aesthetics, piecesGamesVideo games, board gamesCarsModels, brands, dream carsSportsTeams, athletes, eventsCustomYou define your own categories
Each item can be tagged as:

Experienced : watched, read, listened to, visited
Save for Later : want to explore
Favourited : the ones that really stuck

Explore Mode
Don't know where to start? Browse curated options within any category and swipe through them. Found something you forgot you loved? Add it to Experienced. Something new catches your eye? Save it for later.
Powered by open APIs:

Movies & TV -> TMDB
Books -> Google Books API
Music -> Spotify API
Games -> IGDB
Food & Restaurants -> Yelp / Google Places

Time Capsule
Select any time period and see a snapshot of what you were into. Your taste as a timeline, not just a list.
"What was I watching in January 2025?" "What music was I into last summer?"
Social Layer (Stage 2)
Add friends who use Faza and unlock a shared experience:

Recommendations : send a friend something you think they'd love
Activities : plan what to watch, eat, or do together with group voting
Hangout Events : create a shared event and upload photos after
Interest-Based Group Chats : conversation starters and interesting facts sent automatically to keep things flowing

Trusted Ratings (Stage 3)
See how things are rated across three degrees of social proximity:

Your ratings : your personal score
Friends' ratings : people you know directly
Friends of friends : one step removed, still trustworthy

Like Google reviews, but filtered through people you actually know.
Discovery & Matching (Stage 4)
Match with new people, friends or romantically, based purely on shared taste. Choose your degree of separation: direct friends, friends of friends, or wider.
No swiping on photos. Just genuine compatibility through what you love.

Why Faza?
Most apps optimize for consumption. Faza optimizes for memory. It's not about watching more or reading more, it's about holding onto the things that matter to you, and sharing them with the people who get it.
